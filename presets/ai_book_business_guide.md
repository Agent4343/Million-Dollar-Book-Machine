## SECTION 1: THE BUSINESS MODEL OVERVIEW

This system runs on two engines that feed each other. The first engine is your own book production — you use AI to write practical how-to guides and sell them on Amazon's Kindle Direct Publishing platform. The second engine is teaching others to do exactly what you're doing — and charging them for that knowledge. Both engines are built on the same foundation, which means you build once and earn twice.

### How the Two Streams Work Together
Every book you produce for yourself is simultaneously a demonstration piece for your course. You show students exactly how you made it, what prompts you used, how long it took, and what it earned. That transparency builds trust and sells your program better than any marketing copy ever could.

### The Core Business Model at a Glance
- Stream 1: Write AI-assisted how-to guides → Upload to Amazon KDP → Earn royalties 24/7
- Stream 2: Package your process into a course → Sell it to others → Earn course fees
- Stream 3 (optional): Offer done-for-you book production as a premium service
- Stream 4 (optional): Monthly membership community for ongoing student support

### Why This Works — Your Unfair Advantage
You already think in procedures. After 15+ years writing work instructions, procedures, and operational documentation, writing a clear step-by-step guide for a homeowner or beginner athlete is the same skill applied to a different audience. Most people trying to sell how-to content have no idea how to structure information clearly. You do.

Add to that your real hands-on knowledge across trades, sports, mechanics, and operations — and you have content that is genuinely accurate and practical, which is rare in the AI content space. The books that fail on Amazon are thin, generic, and wrong. Yours won't be.

## SECTION 2: SETTING UP YOUR ACCOUNTS

Before you write a single word, get your platforms registered. This takes one afternoon and is completely free.

### Step 1 — Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP)
1. Go to kdp.amazon.com
2. Click 'Sign in' — use your existing Amazon account or create a new one
3. Complete your author/publisher profile (use your real name or create a pen name)
4. Enter your tax information (SIN for Canadian residents — Amazon withholds 0% for Canadians with proper forms on file)
5. Enter your banking information for royalty payments
6. You're done — your account is ready to publish

**PRO TIP**: Set up a separate business bank account before entering banking details. This keeps your royalty income separate from personal funds and makes taxes dramatically simpler at year end.

## SECTION 3: YOUR MASTER BOOK PRODUCTION PROMPT

This is the core engine of your book production system. Copy this prompt, fill in the bracketed sections for each new book, and run it. Every book follows the same structure — professional, thorough, and optimized for Amazon sales.

### The Master Prompt — Book Production Mode

```
You are an expert technical writer specializing in practical, beginner-friendly
how-to guides for the Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) market. Your writing style
is clear, conversational, and action-oriented — like a knowledgeable friend
explaining something over coffee, not a textbook. No fluff, no filler,
no unnecessary jargon.

## THE BOOK I WANT YOU TO WRITE:

Topic: [e.g. How to Install a Kitchen Backsplash Tile]
Target Reader: [e.g. Canadian homeowner, no prior experience, wants to save on labour costs]
Desired Length: [e.g. 40-50 pages / approximately 10,000-12,000 words]
Price Point: [e.g. $9.99]
Tone: [e.g. Practical, encouraging, no-nonsense]

## STRUCTURE TO FOLLOW:

Produce the complete book in this order:

1. TITLE AND SUBTITLE
   - Main title: punchy and benefit-driven
   - Subtitle: explains exactly what the reader gets
   - Generate 3 title options for me to choose from

2. BOOK DESCRIPTION (for Amazon listing)
   - 150-200 words written to sell the book on Amazon
   - Lead with the reader's pain point
   - List 5 bullet point benefits
   - End with a clear call to action

3. INTRODUCTION (500-700 words)
   - Hook the reader with a relatable scenario
   - Explain who this book is for
   - Tell them what they will be able to do by the end
   - Set realistic expectations (time, difficulty, cost savings)
   - Brief roadmap of chapters

4. CHAPTER BY CHAPTER CONTENT
   - Write each chapter FULLY — do not summarize or skip
   - Each chapter: 800-1,500 words
   - Start each chapter with a one-sentence objective
   - Use short paragraphs (3-4 sentences max)
   - Include Pro Tips, Warnings, and Common Mistakes per chapter
   - Use numbered steps for all procedural content
   - End each chapter with 3-5 key takeaways

5. TROUBLESHOOTING SECTION
   - List 8-12 common beginner problems
   - Format: Symptom → Likely Cause → Fix
   - Write as Q&A format

6. TOOLS AND MATERIALS REFERENCE LIST
   - Complete list with notes on each item
   - Note Canadian vs US product name differences
   - Include approximate costs in CAD

7. CONCLUSION (300-400 words)
   - Celebrate completion
   - Remind them what they have accomplished
   - Soft prompt to leave an Amazon review

8. BONUS QUICK-REFERENCE CHEAT SHEET
   - One page summary of the most critical steps
   - Designed to be printed and kept handy

## WRITING RULES:
- Write for Grade 8-10 reading level
- Use "you" throughout — speak directly to the reader
- Metric AND imperial measurements (Canadian audience)
- Never assume prior knowledge — explain every term first use
- Include Canadian context (codes, product availability, climate)
- Every chapter: minimum one Pro Tip and one Common Mistake callout
- Short sentences. Active voice. Always.
- No filler phrases like "In this chapter we will..."

## WHEN COMPLETE:
- Confirm approximate word count
- Flag sections needing photos with [INSERT IMAGE: description]
- Give 5 Amazon SEO keyword phrases
- Suggest 3 related book topics for a series

## MY PERSONAL KNOWLEDGE AND EXPERIENCE:
[ADD YOUR OWN NOTES HERE — real tips, things you learned the hard way,
local knowledge, warnings from experience. This is what makes your book
different from generic AI content.]
```

**CRITICAL**: The 'My Personal Knowledge' section at the bottom is the most important part of this prompt. Even 3-5 bullet points of real experience transforms a generic guide into something genuinely valuable. Never skip it.

When you switch your app into Teaching Mode, the same prompt framework generates course content instead of a reader-facing guide. The output becomes lesson scripts, student exercises, and module content rather than chapter prose.

Course Title: [e.g. AI Book Business — From Idea to Published in 7 Days]
Module Number and Title: [e.g. Module 3 — Formatting Your Book for KDP]
Target Student: [e.g. Complete beginner, no publishing experience,
                wants passive income, has basic computer skills]
Lesson Length: [e.g. 15-20 minute video lesson / 2,500-3,500 words of script]
Delivery Format: [e.g. Video script / Written lesson / Workbook]

## STRUCTURE TO FOLLOW:

1. MODULE INTRO (1-2 minutes / ~200 words)
   - What the student will be able to DO after this module
   - Why this module matters in the bigger picture
   - A quick win to build momentum immediately

2. LESSON CONTENT (broken into sub-lessons if needed)
   - Written as a spoken video script (natural, conversational)
   - Step-by-step walkthroughs with exact clicks and actions
   - Explain the WHY behind each step, not just the what
   - Pause points: [PAUSE — show screen here] markers throughout
   - Common sticking points students face and how to push through

3. DEMONSTRATION SECTIONS
   - Mark as [DEMO: describe exactly what to show on screen]
   - Include the exact words to say while demonstrating

4. STUDENT EXERCISE / HOMEWORK
   - One concrete action the student takes RIGHT NOW
   - Clear deliverable (not vague — specific output they produce)
   - Estimated time to complete

5. MODULE SUMMARY
   - 3-5 bullet point recap of what was covered
   - Bridge sentence leading into the next module

6. DOWNLOADABLE RESOURCE (if applicable)
   - Template, checklist, or worksheet the student keeps
   - Designed to be printed or saved

## TEACHING RULES:
- Write exactly as you would SPEAK — contractions, casual language
- Use "you" throughout — this is a 1:1 conversation
- No academic language — this is practical skills, not theory
- Every module must produce a tangible output the student can show
- Quick wins in every module — momentum is everything
- Acknowledge when something is confusing and explain it twice different ways
- Always tell students what to do if they get stuck

## COURSE POSITIONING:
[Describe your own experience and credentials briefly —
what you have done, how many books you have published,
what you earn. Students buy from people who have done it,
not people who just read about it.]

## SECTION 5: FROM PROMPT TO PUBLISHED — THE FULL WORKFLOW

Here is the exact step-by-step process from having an idea to a published book earning royalties on Amazon. Follow this sequence every time.

### Phase 1 — Research and Topic Selection (30-60 minutes)

Before you write anything, confirm the topic has buyers. Many people skip this and write books nobody searches for.

1. Go to Amazon.ca and type your topic into the search bar
2. Look at the top 10 results — are books selling? Check their Best Seller Rank (BSR) in the product description. Under 100,000 BSR means it sells regularly
3. Check the reviews — under 50 reviews on competing books means the market isn't too saturated
4. Look at pricing — what are others charging? Match or undercut by $1-2 to start
5. Confirm your topic in Google Trends — is there consistent search interest year-round?

**PRO TIP**: The best niches are topics where someone has a problem RIGHT NOW. Tiling a kitchen, fixing a leaky tap, learning to snowboard before a trip. Urgency drives purchases. Evergreen topics (not tied to trends) sell consistently for years.

### Phase 2 — Content Generation (2-4 hours)

1. Open your AI tool (Claude or ChatGPT)
2. Paste your Master Book Production Prompt
3. Fill in all bracketed fields — topic, audience, length, tone
4. Add your personal knowledge notes at the bottom — be specific
5. Run the prompt and review the output
6. Edit for accuracy — especially any technical details that need verification
7. Add any personal stories or examples that only you can provide

**WARNING**: Never publish AI-generated content without reading every word yourself. AI makes plausible-sounding errors, especially around measurements, product codes, and safety information. You are the expert — your review is non-negotiable.

### Phase 3 — Formatting for KDP (1-2 hours)

1. Paste your content into Microsoft Word or Google Docs
2. Set page size to 6x9 inches (the standard KDP trade paperback size)
3. Set margins: 0.75 inches on all sides, 0.875 inch inside margin for binding
4. Use Heading 1 for chapter titles, Heading 2 for sub-sections — this auto-generates your table of contents
5. Add a Table of Contents at the front using Word's automatic TOC feature
6. Insert page numbers in the footer
7. Add front matter: Title page, Copyright page, Disclaimer page
8. Export as PDF for print version, EPUB for e-book version

#### Required Front Matter for Every Book
- Title Page — Book title, subtitle, and your author name
- Copyright Page — Year, your name/pen name, 'All rights reserved', country of first publication
- Disclaimer — 'This book is for informational purposes only. The author makes no warranties...'
- Table of Contents — Auto-generated from your headings in Word

### Phase 4 — Cover Design (30-60 minutes)

1. Go to Canva.com — search 'Kindle book cover' in templates
2. Choose a clean, bold template that fits your topic
3. Update the title, subtitle, and author name
4. Use a high-quality royalty-free image from Unsplash or Pexels
5. Export at 2560 x 1600 pixels minimum, JPG or TIFF format
6. For print: use KDP's Cover Creator tool — it calculates spine width automatically based on your page count

### Phase 5 — Publishing on KDP (45-60 minutes)

1. Log into kdp.amazon.com and click 'Create New Title'
2. Fill in all metadata: title, subtitle, author name, description, keywords, categories
3. Upload your manuscript file (PDF or EPUB)
4. Upload your cover file
5. Use KDP's Previewer to check formatting on multiple device types
6. Set your price — $9.99 for most how-to guides hits the sweet spot
7. Enroll in KDP Select (gives you access to Kindle Unlimited readers and promotional tools)
8. Click Publish — your book goes live within 24-72 hours

**PRO TIP**: Choose two categories for your book — the second category is often overlooked. Pick one broad category and one niche category. Books can reach #1 in niche categories with very few sales, which earns you the 'Best Seller' badge and boosts visibility.

## SECTION 6: AMAZON SEO — GETTING FOUND

Publishing the book is only half the job. If Amazon can't find your book when someone searches for it, it doesn't matter how good it is.

### Keywords — The Most Important Factor

Amazon gives you 7 keyword fields. Use every one of them. These are not single words — they are phrases people actually type into the Amazon search bar.

- Think like a frustrated person with a problem: 'how to tile a backsplash without experience'
- Use long-tail phrases (4-7 words) rather than single words — less competition, more targeted buyer
- Include variations: 'kitchen backsplash tile', 'subway tile installation guide', 'backsplash for beginners'
- Never repeat words already in your title — Amazon already indexes those automatically
- Use the KDP keyword field to add phrases, not just single words — each field accepts up to 50 characters

### Your Book Description — This Is Your Sales Page

Most people write a book description like a synopsis. That's wrong. Your description is an advertisement.

**Book Description Formula:**
- Line 1-2: Identify the exact pain point ('Hiring a tiler costs $800-$2,000. You're about to do it yourself for under $200.')
- Line 3-5: Promise the result ('In this step-by-step guide, you will...')
- Bullet list: 5-7 specific things they will learn or be able to do
- Final line: Call to action ('Scroll up and click Buy Now — your kitchen transformation starts today.')

### Reviews — The Accelerant

Books with reviews sell. Books without reviews stagnate.

- Include a soft ask at the end of your book
- Tell people in your network the book is live
- Enroll in KDP Select and run a free promotion
- Never buy fake reviews — Amazon's detection bans accounts permanently

1. Launch your full course publicly
2. Set up your community platform
3. Run your first KDP free promotion on your oldest book to generate reviews
4. Analyze which books are earning most — produce more in that category
